Most of what I learn at work never gets written down. A tricky migration, a test that finally caught the bug, a prompt that made a RAG answer reliable: it's obvious in the moment and gone a month later.

So I'm starting a daily log here. One post a day, short on purpose.

## What to expect

- **Backend design**: APIs in FastAPI and NestJS, data modelling in PostgreSQL, and the trade-offs behind them.
- **LLMs in real products**: retrieval, evaluation and the boring parts that make a chatbot trustworthy.
- **Delivery**: testing with Jest, CI/CD, Docker and Terraform.
- **Working in Germany**: notes on building software for public-sector and social projects.

## The rules I'm giving myself

1. Write about something I actually did or read that day.
2. Keep it under ten minutes to read.
3. Include code or a concrete example whenever possible.

Some posts will be in German, some in English, depending on the topic. If you want to follow along, there's an [RSS feed](/rss.xml).
